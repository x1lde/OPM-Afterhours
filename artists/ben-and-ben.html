Ben&Ben

About the artist Scroll to read

Ben&Ben is a nine-piece folk-pop band from Manila, led by twin singer-songwriters Paolo Benjamin and Miguel Benjamin Guico. Its ensemble includes acoustic and electric guitars, bass, violin, keyboards, drums, and percussion. Sony Music Philippines describes its sound as a meeting of folk, world, and indigenous musical styles with pop songwriting, supported by layered harmonies and a full-band approach to performance.

The debut studio album Limasawa Street arrived in 2019, followed by Pebble House, Vol. 1: Kuwaderno and The Traveller Across Dimensions. Songs such as Pagtingin and Araw-Araw remain part of its recorded identity. In 2026, Duyan continued a connected series of love songs, while new Reimagined editions revisited Lifetime and Autumn. These releases give listeners a way to follow how the band develops familiar stories and arrangements across different stages of its catalog.
