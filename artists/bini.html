BINI

About the artist Scroll to read

BINI is an eight-member Filipino pop group developed through ABS-CBN’s Star Hunt Academy. Aiah, Colet, Maloi, Gwen, Stacey, Mikha, Jhoanna, and Sheena made their official launch in 2021 with Born To Win. Their group identity brings singing and coordinated choreography together, with recordings that give listeners access to both Filipino-language pop and English-language material.

The catalog includes the albums Born To Win, Feel Good, and FLAMES, alongside releases such as Pantropiko, Karera, and the Talaarawan EP. In 2026, the six-track Signals EP brought together Blush, Unang Kilig, and Step Back, the three selections featured here. These songs provide a focused introduction to that era while retaining different musical and performance approaches. Through its releases and live work, BINI has helped introduce a wider audience to contemporary Filipino group pop.
