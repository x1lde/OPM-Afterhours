No. 10 · Billboard Philippines · first half of 2026

Rob Deniel

Rob Deniel is a Filipino singer-songwriter known for a retro-inspired approach to contemporary pop. His early song Ulap grew from experiments in his bedroom, with the recording made on a phone. In an interview with Billboard Philippines, he explained that the finished song stayed close to its first demo, giving listeners a glimpse of the direct process behind his early recordings.

Later releases include Ang Pag-ibig, RomCom, and Miss Miss, each expanding the catalog beyond that first introduction. RomCom’s music video revisits scenes from Filipino romantic-comedy films, connecting his pop sound with familiar local cinema. His work brings nostalgic references into songs made for his own generation, while maintaining a personal role in writing and production. The three selections here follow that development from Ulap to two of his 2023 releases.
