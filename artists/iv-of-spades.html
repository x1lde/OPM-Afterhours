IV OF SPADES

IV OF SPADES is a Filipino band formed in 2014, bringing together Unique Salonga, Zild Benitez, Blaster Silonga, and Badjao de Castro. Its members draw from a broad range of musical interests, which they have described collectively as “chop suey rock.” That phrase captures a willingness to combine styles rather than settle within one narrow genre.

Mundo became a major early release in 2018. After Unique left to pursue solo work, the remaining trio continued before entering a hiatus in 2020. The original quartet reunited in 2025, releasing Aura and the album Andalucia. Kabisado is another selection from that record. This profile places the earlier single alongside the reunion-era recordings, giving listeners a route through both periods and showing how the band’s shared identity has continued alongside its members’ individual musical careers.
