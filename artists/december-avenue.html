December Avenue

December Avenue is a Filipino rock band that formed in 2007 while its members were studying at the University of Santo Tomas. The group first used the name Sense and Sound before adopting a name linked to moving forward into a new chapter. Its early recordings and regular live performances built an audience for melodic alternative rock and emotionally accessible songwriting.

The self-titled debut album appeared in 2016, followed by Langit Mong Bughaw in 2019. Recordings such as Bulong and Huling Sandali connect the band’s musical catalog with narrative music videos made alongside Tower of Doom and 8th Street Cinema. Saksi Ang Langit, written by Zel Bautista and Jet Danao, later extended that storytelling approach. Across these releases, December Avenue pairs band-driven arrangements with themes of relationships, change, and the people who remain beside us.
