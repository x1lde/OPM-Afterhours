SB19

SB19 is a five-member Filipino pop group comprising Pablo, Josh, Stell, Ken, and Justin. The group debuted in October 2018 with Tilaluha and reached a wider audience through its second single, Go Up. Its name uses “SB” for “Sound Break,” reflecting an ambition to bring Filipino music and culture to listeners beyond the Philippines.

The members manage their work through 1Z Entertainment, a company they established themselves. Releases such as GENTO and the 2025 EP Simula at Wakas combine group vocals, rap, and performance-led pop production. DAM and DUNGKA! introduce two sides of that EP, while the 2026 single VISA addresses barriers faced by Filipinos traveling overseas. SB19’s catalog connects polished group performance with locally rooted writing, making it an important entry point for listeners exploring the development of contemporary P-pop.
