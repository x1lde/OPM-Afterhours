Skusta Clee

About the artist Scroll to read

Skusta Clee is a Filipino hip-hop artist whose recordings move across rap, pop, R&B, and rock-adjacent ballads. His versatility is a central subject of Billboard Philippines’ interview with him and frequent collaborator Flow G. Rather than working within a single sound, he uses both melodic singing and rap to connect with listeners across different corners of contemporary Filipino music.

Kalimutan Ka introduced a rock-oriented direction to audiences familiar with his pop and hip-hop releases. Other recent recordings include Bebe, an R&B-infused pop single, and Since Day One, his collaboration with Flow G. The contrast between Kalimutan Ka and Since Day One makes these project selections a useful starting point for exploring his range. His performance with Jay Durias also connects his own songs with an older Filipino R&B tradition, showing another part of his musical range.
