No. 03 · Billboard Philippines · first half of 2026

fitterkarma

fitterkarma is a Manila-based independent art rock band that developed from Joao de Leon’s solo project into a full group in 2023. Its members met as Music Production students at De La Salle-College of Saint Benilde. De Leon handles vocals and guitar, joined by bassist and backing vocalist Soph Miranda, drummer Sanders Bayas, and guitarist Mikee Mendoza, also known as LORY.

The group works with dark imagery, unusual storytelling, and arrangements that move between acoustic and electric textures. Kalapastangan appeared in 2023, followed by Pag-Ibig ay Kanibalismo II in 2025; both later attracted substantial attention on Philippine charts. The 2026 single maryclaire adds a live string quartet to this musical vocabulary. In interviews, the band emphasizes creative growth rather than repeating a successful formula, bringing an exploratory approach to the wider conversation around Filipino rock.
