## Method and limits

Facts come from artist/label pages, artist interviews, licensed platform listings, and official upload descriptions. Spotify artist/album listings were inspected through their public HTML metadata using an external development tool; only ordinary track anchors are shipped. YouTube oEmbed responses confirmed the exact title and publishing channel for every added video destination. Search-index descriptions supplied additional release and credit evidence. A destination match does not establish local media rights or guarantee regional playback. Some YouTube pages returned cache/fetch errors to the research browser; all added video URLs separately returned successful oEmbed identity responses.

Release dates describe the recording, not its video upload. Years are used for conflicting or incomplete dates (Pagsamo, Ulap, Unang Kilig). Platform reissues can have newer dates than the first release. Artist/label biographies sometimes contain outdated or inconsistent details: December Avenue’s Tower of Doom page calls it four-piece but lists five members; no current size is asserted. Awards, streaming totals, and uncertain personal details were omitted.

The short song descriptions summarize documented recording context, release history, and video concepts. They do not assert private intentions. The introductory comparisons and listening routes in biographies are editorial framing. No unsupported lyrical interpretations are presented.
