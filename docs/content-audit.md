## Missing assets and decisions

- All 10 artist portraits need an image with established identity and reproduction rights.
- 28 selected tracks need local recordings and covers if local hosting is desired. Official links already provide an alternative. Optional videos and lyrics are not required.
- Skusta Clee slot three needs approval of a title and exact recording before source, link, and asset integration. Bebe is recommended in content-sources.md only.
- No new photographic or commercial media was downloaded. Multo assets retain inherited owner authorization, not an independently verified license.
- Unverified songwriting/production credits were omitted, rather than guessed. Some original release dates use verified years to avoid conflicts with upload/reissue dates.
- Exact official YouTube uploads remain unresolved for maryclaire, Kalimutan Ka, and Ulap. Their direct Spotify links are verified. A falsely labeled Kalimutan Ka reupload was excluded.
- Spotify public metadata can map the same recording to multiple releases. The selected destinations match the artist/title and exact version labels; Reimagined, acoustic, live, and stripped-down alternatives were checked explicitly.
- Site language is English; Filipino song titles and names remain in their original spelling. No lyrics are reproduced.
