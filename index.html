About the project

A little room for OPM.

OPM Afterhours is a school music discovery project. Read artist stories, explore featured-song notes, and follow verified links to official recordings. Listen to the supplied Multo recording with the browser’s own controls.

Artist order follows Billboard Philippines’ first-half 2026 list, recorded in the original project as published July 24, 2026. This is a mid-year list, not a final full-year ranking.

Explore 30 selected songs through their cover artwork and official Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube destinations. Multo also has a local audio player. Local music stops when you leave its page; platform links open in a new tab.
