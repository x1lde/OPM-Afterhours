03 / Behind the music

A little room.
A lot of feeling.

OPM Afterhours is a music discovery website made for the moments when you want to slow down and listen. It brings ten Filipino artists and thirty selected songs together in one intimate, thoughtfully arranged space.

Explore the people behind the sound, get to know a recording, and follow a song wherever it takes you. From familiar favorites to a voice you have yet to meet, there is always something worth another listen.

01 / Who

Listeners, like you.

We are a team of student researchers, programmers, and designers sharing an appreciation for Filipino music. This school project brings our different skills together around one shared subject: the songs and artists of OPM.

02 / What

Your next discovery.

A small collection with room to explore. Each artist has a listening room with three featured tracks, cover art, an artist story, song information, and links to Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube.

03 / Why

Keep OPM close.

Music can hold a memory, give a feeling a name, or make an ordinary evening special. We want to make discovering Filipino artists inviting, and give their stories as much attention as their sound.

The inspiration

The warmth of a record.
The stillness of a late night.

The design takes its cue from vinyl sleeves, quiet listening spaces, and the soft glow of a room after dark. Charcoal surfaces, dusty rose accents, translucent glass, and gently turning records give digital music a familiar, tactile setting.

Our artist directory follows Billboard Philippines’ first-half 2026 list . The three songs on each page are project selections, an invitation to explore further.

Make yourself at home

A few ways to settle in.

How do I start listening?

Choose an artist, pick one of the three mini records, and press play on the audio controls. Pause the current song before choosing another. Open Song information for release notes and credits.

Can I listen on my favorite platform?

Yes. Every track has links to Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube that open in a new tab. Local playback ends when you leave an artist page.

Prefer a quieter screen?

Turn on Still mode in the header to hide background videos and stop decorative animation on the current page. The site also follows your device’s reduced-motion preference. Audio controls stay available.
