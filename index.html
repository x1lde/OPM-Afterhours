About the project

A little room for OPM.

OPM Afterhours is a school music discovery project. Browse artist biographies, explore three featured-track slots per artist, and listen using the browser’s controls when a supplied recording is available.

Artist order follows Billboard Philippines’ first-half 2026 list, recorded in the original project as published July 24, 2026. This is a mid-year list, not a final full-year ranking.

Only Multo currently has supplied playable media. Other recordings and artist portraits remain unavailable. Each artist has a dedicated page; music stops when you leave that page.
